# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, constructive and focused on improving BountyLens.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Being kind and patient in reviews.
- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels.
- Assuming good intent while still addressing problems clearly.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive comments that derail project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from the project.

If you see a problem, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
